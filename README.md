# 🛂 PassKit Pro: All-in-One Passport, Visa & Profile Suite

**PassKit Pro** is a fast, lightweight, and privacy-focused client-side web application designed to format, calibrate, and prepare document assets for official government and visa portal submissions.

🔗 **Live Demo:** ((https://quickpassportphoto.netlify.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

* **📷 Auto-Crop & Passport Background Engine**
  * **Standard Indian Passport:** 35 × 45 mm (3.5 × 4.5 cm / 7:9 ratio)
  * **Global Square Standard:** 40 × 40 mm (4 × 4 cm / 1:1 ratio)
  * **US Visa / Indian OCI:** 51 × 51 mm (2 × 2 inches)
  * **Background Replacement:** Instant switch between Royal Blue, Sky Blue, Pure White, Off-White/Grey, and Red.
  * **300 DPI Export:** High-resolution single photo and 8-photo printable grid sheet generator.

* **✍️ Signature Formatter**
  * Dimension lock with real-time conversion between **Pixels (px)** and **Inches (in)**.
  * Custom aspect bounding box preview for government portal upload compliance.

* **📅 Accurate DOB & Age Calculator**
  * Calculates exact age breakdown down to the day (Years, Months, Days).
  * Total cumulative lifetime days lived.
  * Exact countdown to the upcoming birthday.

## 🛠️ Built With

* **HTML5 Canvas API:** High-resolution pixel manipulation, dynamic cropping, and backdrop rendering.
* **Tailwind CSS:** Modern glassmorphism UI and responsive design.
* **Vanilla JavaScript:** Fast, dependency-free client-side execution.
* **Lucide Icons:** Clean, minimalist iconography.
